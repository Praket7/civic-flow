CivicFlowFBLA · Coding & Programming

Nonprofit volunteer management

More time for service. Less time managing it.

CivicFlow connects volunteer opportunities, shift staffing, check-ins, verified hours and reporting. Its offline-first desktop application uses an explainable scheduling optimizer called AutoRoster.

Explore interactive sampleInspect source code

Sample data, not a live nonprofit. The interactive browser demo saves changes only in your browser. It does not create real hosted accounts, synchronize real records or substitute for the independently installed offline desktop application. Public installers are not advertised until verified.

One connected workflow

Every open shift has a clear path to being filled.

Behind every community program are dozens of small decisions. CivicFlow brings them into one calm, connected workspace—from the first staffing need through verified service.

01 / Plan

A clearer picture of the week

Programs, shift capacity, staffing gaps, and volunteer availability come together in a schedule that stays useful as plans change.

02 / Match

Intelligence with an explanation

AutoRoster weighs skills, availability, and coverage requirements. It shows why candidates qualify while keeping final decisions with the coordinator.

03 / Verify

Service that can be accounted for

Attendance, approved hours, and configurable reports connect daily coordination with outcomes an organization can review.

Built with intention

Thoughtful details. Dependable decisions.

CivicFlow pairs a focused interface with careful data rules, reproducible staffing logic, and reporting that can be traced to underlying records.

Data integrity

Precise by design

Capacity and scheduling rules reject impossible values before they become operational problems. Changes are reflected throughout the connected workspace.

Human oversight

Recommendations, never guesswork

Matching constraints and candidate explanations make AutoRoster transparent. Every proposed roster remains subject to human review.

Evidence

Reports grounded in records

Flexible date ranges, program filters, and exportable fields turn service data into useful analysis without confusing a brief preview with the full report.
