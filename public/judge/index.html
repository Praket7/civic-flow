01 / The daily work

Every open shift has a clear path to being filled.

From the first staffing request to the final hours report, coordinators can follow the same records instead of copying them between spreadsheets.

01 — Plan

See where help is needed.

Build opportunities and shifts, check staffing gaps, and track registrations and waitlists.

02 — Match

Find someone who fits.

Compare eligible volunteers against role requirements, availability and existing commitments before accepting a roster.

03 — Verify

Account for the work.

Check attendance, review service hours and export reports from the underlying records.

02 / Inside the optimizer

Staffing is a constraint problem.

A person can be willing to help and still be a poor fit for a particular shift. AutoRoster separates eligibility from ranking, then leaves the decision with the coordinator.

Follow a proposed assignment

Open each step to see how a staffing recommendation is built. This is an explanation of the algorithm, not a live benchmark.

01 · Establish eligibility

Check role skills, credential validity on the shift date, age rules and availability. Reject candidates who fail required constraints.

02 · Evaluate the remaining candidates

Consider workload, preferences, existing commitments and policy settings. The ranking is only meaningful after eligibility is established.

03 · Build a plan

Fill open seats while respecting the selected scheduling constraints. Track unfilled seats and explain why a candidate was chosen.

04 · Review before applying

The coordinator reviews the assignments and their explanations. A proposal is not an automatic change to the roster.
